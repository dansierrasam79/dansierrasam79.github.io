1. Overview and Core Privacy Principle

Sheet Data Cleaner Studio is engineered on a strict zero-data-collection, client-side execution model. The Add-on executes 100% deterministic data transformation algorithms locally inside your Google Apps Script V8 runtime session. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your spreadsheet data, personal information, or user files.

2. Permissions and Scopes Used

The Add-on requests strictly narrow, essential OAuth scopes required solely to perform spreadsheet operations within the active document:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly: Allows the Add-on to read and format data only in the specific spreadsheet where you activate it. It cannot view, access, or alter any other files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui: Allows the Add-on to render its sidebar interface and native dialog prompts within Google Sheets.

3. Data Processing Architecture

  • In-Memory Processing: All data transformations (whitespace sanitization, duplicate identification, date standardization, casing changes, missing value handling, and PII masking) run entirely in-memory within your active browser session and Google's execution sandbox.
  • Zero External Transmission: The Add-on contains zero external webhooks, telemetries, third-party analytics trackers, or external database calls. Your cell data never leaves Google's ecosystem.
  • No Artificial Intelligence or LLM Processing: The Add-on operates on pure deterministic regular expressions and standard algorithms. No user data is sent to external artificial intelligence models or used for model training.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Because the Add-on does not operate external databases or servers, no user data, spreadsheet contents, tokens, or profile information is ever stored, logged, or retained by us.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties. All operations are strictly confined between your Google account and Google Cloud's official Apps Script infrastructure.

6. Google Limited Use Policy Compliance

Sheet Data Cleaner Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
