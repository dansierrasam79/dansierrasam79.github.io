Looker Studio Community Connector

Open-Meteo Weather Data

Zero-server, keyless Google Looker Studio Community Connector streaming historical ERA5 reanalysis and daily weather forecast metrics directly from Open-Meteo.

Connect in Looker Studio View Template Report

Keyless Public Access

Uses AuthTypes.NONE with zero API key setup. Every Looker Studio viewer queries Open-Meteo's public REST APIs directly under their own session.

Automatic Archive & Forecast Routing

Seamlessly splits queries across archive-api.open-meteo.com (ERA5 historical reanalysis) and api.open-meteo.com (recent & forecast) at the 5-day reanalysis lag boundary.

Zero-Server & Multi-Location Ready

Runs 100% inside Google Apps Script V8 with strict script.external_request scope, SHA-256 ScriptCache deduplication, and overridable coordinate/unit parameters.

Semantic Schema Reference

The connector exposes 9 deterministic fields (2 dimensions and 7 daily weather metrics) for time-series trajectories, KPI scorecards, and multi-location blending:

Field Name Field ID Type Description
Date date Date (YYYYMMDD) Observation or forecast date formatted for continuous Looker Studio timelines.
Location location Text Configured location label echoed for multi-city slicing and dashboard blending.
Max Temperature (2m) temperature_2m_max Number (AVG) Maximum daily air temperature at 2 meters above ground (°C or °F).
Min Temperature (2m) temperature_2m_min Number (AVG) Minimum daily air temperature at 2 meters above ground (°C or °F).
Mean Temperature (2m) temperature_2m_mean Number (AVG) Mean daily air temperature at 2 meters above ground (°C or °F).
Total Precipitation precipitation_sum Number (SUM) Sum of daily precipitation (rain, showers, snow) in millimeters.
Max Wind Speed (10m) wind_speed_10m_max Number (MAX) Maximum daily wind speed at 10 meters above ground (km/h, m/s, mph, or kn).
Sunshine Duration (Hours) sunshine_duration_hours Number (SUM) Daily sunshine duration converted from raw seconds into hours (2 decimal places).
WMO Weather Code weather_code Number (NONE) Most severe WMO weather interpretation code (0–99) recorded for the day.

Quick Start & Support

  • Step 1: Click Connect in Looker Studio above and authorize the minimal external network permission (script.external_request).
  • Step 2: Enter decimal coordinates (Latitude and Longitude), a friendly Location Label, and your preferred temperature (celsius / fahrenheit) and wind speed units.
  • Step 3: Select any date range in Looker Studio—historical dates automatically route to the ERA5 reanalysis archive while recent and upcoming dates route to the forecast endpoint.
  • Support Contact: For bug reports, feature requests, or integration assistance, reach out via danielchakraborty.com.
