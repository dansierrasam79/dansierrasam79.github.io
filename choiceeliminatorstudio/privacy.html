Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: ChoiceEliminator Studio is engineered under a zero-server, zero-egress architecture. Your form structure, respondent selections, and quota settings never leave your Google Account. We do not operate external databases, run telemetry, or transmit data across the network.

1. Overview and Architecture

ChoiceEliminator Studio ("the add-on", "we", "our") is a Google Workspace editor add-on for Google Forms developed by Daniel Chakraborty. The add-on functions strictly as an in-memory client-side script running via Google Apps Script inside your Google account. All operations occur directly within your form container.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or form response content to external servers. Data processed by the add-on includes:

  • Form Item Schema & Option Strings: Read temporarily to display questions and option choices inside the configuration sidebar.
  • Submission Choice Tallies: Incremented locally to evaluate whether a selected choice has met its configured limit.
  • Configuration Snapshots: Stored locally using Google Apps Script PropertiesService.getDocumentProperties() bound strictly to your active form file to enable one-click restoration.

3. Scopes and Permissions Requested

ChoiceEliminator Studio adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege, requesting only the minimal permissions necessary to operate:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/forms.currentonly: Used strictly to read question choices, modify choice options when capacity is reached, and receive installable form submission trigger events within the specific form where the add-on is executed. The add-on cannot access any other forms in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui: Used solely to render the configuration sidebar interface inside the Google Forms editor.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Transfer

We do not sell, license, share, or transfer your data to any third parties, advertising networks, or external service providers. ChoiceEliminator Studio makes no outbound network connections (no UrlFetchApp calls or external API endpoints).

5. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

ChoiceEliminator Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all configuration data and tallies reside in the Google Forms container's DocumentProperties, deleting the add-on from your form or deleting the Google Form itself immediately and permanently purges all associated configurations. You can also reset all counters and restore options at any time using the "Restore Choices" button inside the sidebar.
