Automate Form Closures Without External Servers

FormLimit Studio is a native Google Workspace™ add-on designed for form owners who require precise submission caps or date/time deadlines. Built entirely inside Google Apps Script™, it features a zero-server architecture where your form configuration and submission data never leave Google's secure infrastructure.

Core Features

  • Response Count Limits: Automatically shuts down the form the exact second a pre-configured response threshold is reached.
  • Scheduled Deadlines: Automatically disables public submissions when a specific date and time occurs.
  • Custom Closed Messages: Tailor the notification message respondents see once the form shuts down.
  • Transactional Email Alerts: Notifies form creators immediately via Gmail™ when a form reaches its limit.
