2. Description of the Service

SheetDiff & Reconciliation Studio is an in-sheet productivity utility designed for Google Sheets. The Add-on allows users to:

  • Select two sheets (Base Sheet and Target Sheet) within the active workbook.
  • Specify single or composite primary key columns to match entities.
  • Perform in-memory row and cell comparisons to identify added, removed, modified, and unchanged rows.
  • Generate a dedicated reconciliation report tab (Diff_Reconciliation_Report) containing an executive summary card and an itemized audit log.
  • Apply non-destructive visual highlight overlays on the Target sheet and clear highlights on demand.

All calculations and comparisons are executed 100% in-memory via the Google Apps Script V8 runtime within your active Google Workspace session. No external servers, webhooks, or third-party APIs are contacted.
