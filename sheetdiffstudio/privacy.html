Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Executive Summary: SheetDiff & Reconciliation Studio executes 100% locally within your active Google Sheets session using the Google Apps Script V8 runtime. We do not operate external web servers, we do not store or transmit your spreadsheet data, and we collect zero user telemetry.

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy governs the use of SheetDiff & Reconciliation Studio (the "Add-on"), developed and operated by Daniel Chakraborty ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), available at danielchakraborty.com. We are committed to building deterministic, privacy-first developer tools that respect end-user data autonomy.

2. Data Collection and Processing

The Add-on functions exclusively as a client-side utility running within the Google Workspace Apps Script execution container:

  • Spreadsheet Cell Data: When you execute a comparison, sheet data is read into temporary, ephemeral system memory in Google's runtime environment to perform array diffing, hash mapping, variance detection, and report tab formatting. None of this data is logged, transmitted, persisted, or cached outside your spreadsheet.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not deploy third-party trackers (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Sentry), advertising beacons, or background diagnostics.
  • No External Server Communication: The Add-on contains no network communication code (such as UrlFetchApp) and connects to no external APIs, third-party databases, or cloud endpoints.
  • No AI/LLM Data Ingestion: The Add-on is 100% deterministic code. Your cell contents and metadata are never used to train, fine-tune, or prompt artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Google OAuth Scopes & Permissions

In strict adherence to the principle of least privilege, SheetDiff & Reconciliation Studio requests only the minimal scopes required to inspect two sheets and generate the reconciliation report:

OAuth Scope Explicit Purpose & Technical Usage
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly Grants read and write access only to the active spreadsheet file in which you manually launch the Add-on. The Add-on reads your selected Base and Target sheets, writes the Diff_Reconciliation_Report tab, and applies visual background highlights on the Target sheet. It cannot view, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui Required solely to render the native sidebar interface (sheet pickers, primary key selector, options checkboxes) and system alert dialogs within Google Sheets via HtmlService.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use)

SheetDiff & Reconciliation Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only use access to read and write data to provide and improve user-facing features directly prominent in the Add-on's user interface.
  • We do not transfer your data to any third party under any circumstances.
  • We do not use or transfer your spreadsheet data for serving advertisements, personalized advertising, retargeting, or interest-based marketing.
  • We do not allow humans to read your spreadsheet data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all processing occurs ephemerally in-memory inside your own Google account session, we do not possess, store, or host any of your data on external infrastructure.

You can revoke the Add-on's access at any time by navigating to your Google Account Permissions and removing access for "SheetDiff & Reconciliation Studio". You can also uninstall the Add-on via the Google Workspace Marketplace at any moment.

6. Security Architecture

The Add-on relies on Google Workspace's secure sandboxed environment. Because there are no developer-managed databases, API keys, or intermediate cloud servers, your spreadsheet data never leaves Google's boundary and is subject to Google's standard enterprise security, access controls, and TLS encryption.

7. Children's Privacy

The Add-on does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Add-on after any revisions constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
