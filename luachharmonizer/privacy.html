Privacy Policy

Application: Luach Workweek & Jewish Calendar Harmonizer • Effective Date: November 7, 2026 • Developer: Daniel Chakraborty

Zero-Telemetry Commitment: Luach Harmonizer is built on a strict privacy-first architecture. All date conversions, holiday detections, and schedule calculations execute 100% locally in-memory within your browser and Google Apps Script runtime. No user data is ever collected, stored, or transmitted to any external server.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Luach Workweek & Jewish Calendar Harmonizer ("Luach Harmonizer", "the Add-on", "we", "us") handles data when you install and use the Add-on within Google Sheets. We respect your digital privacy and adhere strictly to data minimization principles.

2. Data Accessed and Purpose of Use

Luach Harmonizer accesses only the operational data strictly necessary to provide calendar conversions and schedule adjustments:

  • Designated Spreadsheet Cells: When you execute a timeline adjustment or invoke a custom formula (such as =HEBREW_DATE(), =IS_CHAG(), =ISRAEL_WORKDAYS(), or =ISRAEL_WORKDAY_ADD()), the Add-on reads input date and duration values from the specific cells you select or pass as arguments.
  • Calculated Cell Outputs: The Add-on writes calculated Hebrew calendar representations and adjusted Gregorian completion dates directly back to the active spreadsheet.
  • Active Spreadsheet Timezone: The Add-on queries the document's local timezone (via Google Apps Script native methods) solely to prevent unintended calendar day shifts during date conversions.

3. Minimal Google OAuth Scopes

Luach Harmonizer requests only minimal OAuth scopes required for core functionality:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly — Grants access exclusively to the specific spreadsheet document where you explicitly launch or execute the Add-on. It cannot access, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive or Google Sheets account.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui — Allows the Add-on to display user interfaces inside Google Sheets, including the task harmonizer sidebar, demo sheet generation alerts, and configuration dialogs.

4. Data Storage and Retention

We operate zero persistent external storage. Luach Harmonizer does not maintain external databases, caches, logging endpoints, or cloud backends. All computations occur dynamically in runtime memory and terminate when the execution cycle finishes. We do not store, log, or track your identity, Google account details, document contents, or calculation history.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Luach Harmonizer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • User data is used exclusively to provide and improve the user-facing features of the Add-on within the active spreadsheet.
  • User data is never transferred to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • User data is never used for serving advertisements, personalized marketing, or training machine learning models.

6. Third-Party Sharing and Analytics

Luach Harmonizer contains zero third-party tracking libraries, zero web analytics (such as Google Analytics or Mixpanel), and zero advertising SDKs. No personal or spreadsheet data is ever disclosed or transmitted to third parties.
